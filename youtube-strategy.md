# YouTube Strategy — L Starburst / GSA

**Goal:** Generate eyeballs → build brand → drive traffic to LinkedIn → convert to Dr Gus users

**Channel type:** Faceless educational content. No face required.

---

## Content Formats That Work Without Face

| Format | How it works | Effort | Best for |
|--------|-------------|--------|----------|
| **Shorts (9:16, <60s)** | Text-on-screen + stock footage + AI voice | Low | Eyeballs, virality |
| **Screen recordings** | Record your screen + voiceover | Medium | Tutorials, tools |
| **Slideshow** | Images + text + music | Low | Top 5 lists, tips |
| **Animated text** | Moving text + background | Low | Quotes, key insights |
| **Tool demo** | Show Dr Gus or AMICA in action | Medium | Product demos |

**Priority: Shorts first.** YouTube aggressively pushes Shorts to new viewers. One viral Short can bring 10K+ views.

## Production Pipeline (No Face)

### Tool Stack
| Tool | Purpose | Cost |
|------|---------|------|
| **Canva** | Shorts creation (text + stock) | Free |
| **CapCut** | Video editing (mobile/desktop) | Free |
| **ElevenLabs** or **TikTok TTS** | AI voiceover | Free tier |
| **Pexels/Pixabay** | Stock footage | Free |
| **OBS Studio** | Screen recording | Free |
| **HeyGen** | Avatar videos (optional) | Paid |

### Shorts Production Flow (15 min each)
1. Content Engine generates the script/topic
2. Canva: Create 9:16 template with text + stock video
3. CapCut: Add AI voiceover + background music
4. Upload to YouTube Shorts with description + hashtags
5. Cross-post to LinkedIn + Facebook

### Hashtag Strategy
Use 3-5 hashtags per Short:
```
#AITips #Productivity #AIforBusiness #Shorts
```
Mix of: broad (#AI), niche (#AITips), channel-specific (#GSA)
