# HeyGen Digital Twin Avatar Production Plan

## Phase 1: Recording Guide (Instant Avatar)

**What you need to record:** A 2-minute video of yourself talking naturally.

### Setup
- **Camera:** Phone camera at eye level, portrait mode (9:16)
- **Lighting:** Facing a window or ring light — no shadows on face
- **Background:** Plain wall or tidy office — no clutter
- **Audio:** Speak clearly, phone mic is fine. No background noise.
- **Clothing:** Solid colors (no stripes/checks — they cause AI artifacts)

### Script for Recording (read this aloud for 2 minutes)

> "Hi, I'm Josh Lai. I run Global South Advisory, where we help businesses use AI agents to automate their operations.
>
> Over the past year, I've built AI systems that handle lead generation, market research, deal sourcing, and strategic analysis. My AI agent Dr Gus can analyze a $500 million M&A deal and tell you whether to pass with 85% confidence.
>
> The key insight I've learned is this: most companies don't need a bigger AI budget. They need someone who understands both business strategy AND how to build AI agents.
>
> That's what I do. I bridge the gap between strategy and execution.
>
> If you're a business leader who wants AI that actually ships — not just slide decks — let's talk."
>
> *(Total: ~60 seconds — record it twice for 2 minutes total)*

### Camera Angles
1. **Main take (90%):** Look directly at camera lens (not the screen). Speak naturally, as if talking to a colleague.
2. **Hand gestures:** Use natural hand gestures. Don't be stiff.
3. **Pace:** Speak at normal conversation speed. Pause between sentences. No rushing.

### What NOT to do
- ✗ Look at yourself on screen (look at the CAMERA LENS)
- ✗ Read from a script on screen (memorize the flow, not the words)
- ✗ Sudden head movements (stay within frame)
- ✗ Striped or patterned shirts

### Delivery Tips
- Smile at the start and end
- Pause 1 second between each paragraph
- End with a nod

## Phase 2: HeyGen Dashboard Setup (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Upload Avatar Recording
1. Go to **https://app.heygen.com**
2. Log in to your account
3. Click **"My Avatar"** (left sidebar) → **"Instant Avatar"**
4. Click **"Create Instant Avatar"**
5. Upload the 2-minute video you recorded
6. Wait ~1-2 hours for AI processing
7. You'll receive an email when your digital twin is ready: **"Your avatar is ready"**

### Step 2: Verify Avatar
1. Go to **"My Avatar"** → find your new avatar
2. Click **"Preview"** to test
3. Type a test sentence: *"Hello, I'm Josh. Welcome to Global South Advisory."*
4. Click **Generate** → check if the lip-sync and expressions look natural
5. If satisfied, proceed. If not, re-record with better lighting.

### Step 3: Create Your First Video
1. Click **"Create Video"** (top right) or go to **"My Videos"** → **"Create Video"**
2. Under **"Avatar"**, select your new digital twin avatar
3. Under **"Script"**, select **"Text"** mode (not voice cloning — use text first)

### Step 4: Input the Script
Paste this script into the text box:

> *(0-5 seconds)*
> Is your team drowning in repetitive admin work? AI agents are the silent co-workers your business needs.
>
> *(5-25 seconds)*
> These aren't just chatbots. AI agents are autonomous systems that handle scheduling, data entry, invoice reminders, and customer follow-ups. They free your people for high-value work.
>
> *(25-45 seconds)*
> Example: A local bakery used an AI agent to auto-send order confirmations, manage inventory alerts, and schedule social media posts. Result? 15 hours saved per week.
>
> *(45-55 seconds)*
> Start small. Pick one repetitive task. Let an AI agent handle it this month.
>
> *(55-60 seconds)*
> I'm Josh Lai from Global South Advisory. Follow for more AI tips.

### Step 5: Adjust Settings
1. **Background:** Click **"Background"** → choose a clean background or upload your own
2. **Text Style:** Add lower-third text for key points (e.g., "15 hrs/week saved")
3. **Pacing:** Adjust speed if needed (default is fine)
4. **Language:** English

### Step 6: Generate
1. Click **"Submit"** or **"Generate"**
2. Wait 5-15 minutes for rendering
3. Preview the video
4. Click **"Download"** → MP4
