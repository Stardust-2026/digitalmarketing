## Content Format
Each post must follow this exact structure:
- **Hook** (1-2 sentences, question or surprising fact)
- **Explanation** (2-3 sentences, simple, no jargon)
- **Example** (1-2 sentences, concrete, relatable)
- **CTA** (1 sentence, invitation to engage)
- **Hashtags** (3-4 max)

Max 200 words. Beginner-friendly. Business audience.
