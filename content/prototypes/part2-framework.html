Zoom to Application:
Three Lenses

Sun Zi said: "Victorious warriors win first and then go to war."
Here are three lenses to ensure you deploy AI that actually wins.
LENS 01
What can I ship this week?
Not "what's the long-term AI roadmap" — but one agent, one workflow, one automation that delivers visible value in 7 days.
LENS 02
Where is my bottleneck?
80% of AI's value comes from automating the one task that eats your time. Find it. Fix it. Move on.
LENS 03
Does this replace or augment?
The best AI applications don't replace people. They make one person capable of what used to take ten.
